Snake on a 10 x 10 grid (row 1 at the top, column 1 on the left). The snake moves one cell per step; eating food makes it one cell longer. Hitting a wall or its own body ends the game. The game also ends after 1000 steps, or after 200 steps in a row without food.
Heading: up. Length: 3. Food eaten: 0. Step 0 of 1000. Steps since the last food: 0 of 200.
Head at row 5, column 5. Tail at row 7, column 5. Food at row 1, column 3.

--- instructions ---
Choose the snake's move for this step. Each option names the turn, the cell the head enters and what happens there. Goal: eat as much food as possible without hitting a wall or the snake's own body.

--- options ---
[left] turn left: the head moves west to row 5, column 4; the cell is empty
[straight] go straight: the head moves north to row 4, column 5; the cell is empty
[right] turn right: the head moves east to row 5, column 6; the cell is empty
